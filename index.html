About

I build the measurement layer under e-commerce sites, and I make product reels when the data is quiet.

Day to day that means client and server-side tagging, consent that actually gates the tags it should, and clean event streams into the warehouse. Away from the containers it means Blender, light and timing. Both jobs come down to making something invisible behave exactly as intended.
